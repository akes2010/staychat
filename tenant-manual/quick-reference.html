Front desk

One shift, one page — arrivals, departures, payments and the handover.
01of 02
Print A4 · keep at the desk Sidebar order: Dashboard → Rooms → Bookings → Guest Wi-Fi Full detail: manual ch. 01–07

Start of shift 5 checks

  1. Dashboard. Occupancy, revenue today, and any red pending badge on Room Service.
  2. Arrivals. Bookings → Upcoming; note names, rooms, times and any Pending online booking.
  3. Room strip. Dirty rooms must be cleaned before the first check-in — assign cleaning from Rooms.
  4. Handover book. Read the previous shift's notes before you change anything.

Check a guest in 5 steps

  1. Bookings → find the reservation → Check-in. A walk-in gets a reservation first (+ New Reservation, phone first, source Direct/Walk-in), then checks in.
  2. Confirm the name and phone against the ID — never check in a placeholder name.
  3. Check the dates, room and rate on screen, and take a deposit if the folio is not prepaid.
  4. Confirm. The room turns Occupied and the guest appears on the room card.
  5. Hand over the Wi-Fi card and the key; tell housekeeping if the guest needs anything.

Check a guest out money first

  1. Open the booking → Checkout (or the room card → Check-out).
  2. Read the folio: room nights, POS and room-service charges, pool or hall bookings.
  3. Settle any balance. Payments → 💳 Settle a Balance clears the room and every unpaid charge in one action.
  4. Print the A4 folio and hand it to the guest.
  5. Confirm checkout. The room turns Dirty — housekeeping sees it immediately.

Take a payment

MethodWhat you must check
CashEnter the amount received; check the change or shortfall shown.
Card / POS terminalTake the money on the terminal first, then record it here.
Bank transferOnly once the credit is visible — never on a promise.
Payment linkFor remote guests; settle when the link is paid.
Never record a card payment you did not receive. A wrong charge is corrected at its source; refunds need a manager and a written reason.

Move a room / extend a stay

  1. Open the booking → 🔀 Transfer; only rooms free for the whole stay are offered.
  2. Late checkout: ✎ Edit the dates (a manager may be needed for the total). Tell housekeeping.

Guest Wi-Fi 30 seconds

  1. Check-in normally; if a controller is configured, the session is created automatically.
  2. Open Guest Wi-Fi → Credentials; print the card and hand it over.
  3. No session? Open the booking → 📶 Wi-Fi to provision one on demand.
  4. Early departure: Revoke. Late checkout: extend the expiry instead of issuing new credentials.

When something goes wrong

ProblemDo this
No room for a bookingStop. Check confirmation times and deposits before moving anyone. Transfer the later booking, or walk the guest with a manager's approval.
Internet downKeep working: the badge shows Offline and check-ins queue. Do not write a paper receipt as well — two records, one double charge.
Guest disputes a chargeOpen Payments, find the origin and show the itemised detail. Correct at the source; never delete a transaction.
WhatsApp stoppedSettings → WhatsApp: check credentials and Test Connection. Log enquiries with + New Conversation meanwhile.

End of shift handover

  • Every arrival checked in or noted; every departure closed with a zero balance.
  • No room left Dirty without a housekeeping assignment; cash counted and any variance written down.
  • Open issues (maintenance, late arrivals, complaints) in the handover book, with a name against each.
Trainer tip. Practise one check-in, one checkout and one cash settlement until each takes under two minutes. Speed at the desk comes from knowing the next screen, not from skipping a field.

Restaurant & bar POS

Open a ticket, add rounds, settle it correctly, close your till.
02of 02
Sidebar → Restaurant POS / Bar POS Restaurant receipts 80 mm Bar receipts 58 mm Full detail: manual ch. 10–14

Open a ticket or tab

  1. + New Ticket. Table chips show the outlet's tables; a chip with 🔒 already has an open ticket — never seat it twice.
  2. Pick the table or seat, or type a label (“Table 9”, “Bar Seat 2”, “Pool Bar 1”).
  3. Add party size; add the guest name, and link an occupied room if they are in-house.
  4. Open Ticket. In the bar, Close the tab between rounds — it stays on the board with its age and total.
One ticket = one table = one payment story. Add every item before you reach Checkout; the payment screen cannot invent a missing drink.

Add or change items

  1. Tap menu tiles, or search by name. Categories are the pills across the top.
  2. − / + changes quantity; zero removes the line.
  3. Read the receipt panel before checkout: quantity, unit price, line total, service charge and tax.

  1. Apply Discount % only with the approval your hotel requires; totals recalculate live.
  2. Confirm. The ticket closes, the receipt opens on the outlet's paper, and the board updates.
  3. Print or close the popup. The money is now in Payments and the day's outlet total.

Two payers, one table

Split by ticket, not by hope: close one ticket for each payer (for example $40 to Room Charge, $40 by Card). Both then appear separately in the cash-up, and nobody argues about who paid what.

Voids, comps, discounts

SituationCorrect action
Ticket opened by mistake, nothing servedVoid Ticket — no charge is posted.
Food cooked or drink served, guest unhappyDiscount % or comp with a manager's approval, so the cost is visible in reports.
Guest left before orderingVoid the ticket and clear the table.
Price looks wrongDo not improvise at the till. Tell the manager; fix the menu in Menu Manager.

Receipts & reprints

  • 80 mm restaurant, 58 mm bar, A4 at the desk. Pick the device and paper in the print dialog.
  • A reprint is marked “Reprinted copy — original reference …”; it can never pass as an original.
  • Nothing prints? Check Printers: device type and department default. Fix the printer — don't print on the wrong machine.

Close your till

  1. Every ticket on the board must be closed — or carry a written reason.
  2. End of Day → your department → Close. The modal shows system totals by method.
  3. Count your cash and compare. A variance is recorded with a note, never edited into the system total.
  4. Hand the close slip and any variance note to the manager; only a manager can reopen a closed day.

Five mistakes that cost money

  1. Typing the bill total into the cash tendered field.
  2. Room-charging a room that checked out this morning (it will not appear — use Card or Customer Bill).
  3. Voiding a served item instead of discounting it, so the cost disappears from reporting.
  4. Leaving an open tab on the board at close.
  5. Settling a bar tab on the restaurant terminal.
Trainer tip. Rehearse the cash screen until change is automatic. The commonest till error is not the menu — it is the tendered-amount field.
