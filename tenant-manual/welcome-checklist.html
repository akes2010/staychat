Before day one setup · 30–60 min

  • Owner account live. Sign in at /app and change the password that came with the welcome email.
  • Rooms and rates. Every sellable room has a number, type and price; the room strip shows the right count.
  • Staff accounts. One login per person, with the right role. Nobody shares the owner login.
  • WhatsApp connected. Send one test thread and reply from the Inbox.
  • Payments. Cash and card methods on; gateway connected if you take online payments. Take one test payment.
  • Booking page. Open your public link on a phone and make a test booking, then cancel it.
  • Print the pack. Cheat sheet for the desk and till, a manual per department, this sheet per trainee.

First 30 minutes with the team orientation

  • Dashboard tour. Occupancy, arrivals today, and the pending badges they must clear before shift end.
  • The offline rule. Keep working; never write the same booking or payment twice on paper.
  • Hand out the role path. Agree shift 1 watch, shift 2 do, shift 3 own — and the sign-off slot.
  • Show where help lives. Sidebar → Help & training, and the manual chapter for their role.
Give every new hire three things: the manual chapter list for their path, the printed cheat sheet for their station, and a named trainer for the first three shifts. Reading first, clicking second.

Two-week review manager

  • Every role has a signed-off owner for each shift.
  • Re-run one drill per role (manual ch. 80) and note who needs a refresher.
  • Check End of Day closes on time and variances are explained, not edited.
