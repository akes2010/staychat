How a booking moves

From the guest’s phone to your front desk in four steps.

1

Guest books on your page

Live availability priced from your own rate card. Name and phone number only. They pay at the hotel.

2

They get a booking code

A short code like BE-7K2M9QX4 that is easy to read over the phone. The WhatsApp confirmation arrives straight away.

3

Your desk confirms

The booking shows up with the code in front. One click holds the room and tells the guest.

4

Check-in just works

The guest shows the code. Their bill, restaurant tickets and checkout all live in the same system.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Do guests pay online to book?+

No. The booking page is reserve now, pay at the hotel. Guests leave a name and phone number and get a booking code. Deposits can be recorded when they arrive.

How does the booking code work?+

Every online booking gets a short code such as BE-7K2M9QX4. Guests show it at the desk, or send it to the WhatsApp receptionist to check their booking status.

Who pays WhatsApp message fees?+

Meta charges businesses for many WhatsApp messages. Each hotel connects its own WhatsApp Business account directly with Meta, and Meta bills that account — StayChat adds no markup on messaging.

Where is our guest data kept, and can we take it with us?+

Each hotel’s data is kept separate from every other hotel’s. The cloud version runs on our servers with an automatic backup every day and 14 days of restore points, and every report downloads as a CSV you can keep. The Desk PC Edition stores everything on your own machine instead.

What if the internet goes down?+

The app installs on a desk PC or phone and tolerates short drops. The Desk PC Edition runs fully offline on your own network.

What happens after the free trial?+

You choose a plan or stop. No card is taken during the trial. If you do not choose a plan, your data is kept for 30 days so you can export it — every report downloads as a CSV — and then it is deleted.
