FAQ

Questions, answered

Do guests need to pay online to book?
No. The booking engine is "reserve now, pay at the hotel" — guests leave a name and phone number and receive a tender code. Your desk confirms the booking, and payment happens at check-in through the normal folio flow. Deposits and online prepayments can be recorded when they arrive.
How does the tender code work?
Every online booking gets a short code like BE-7K2M9QX4 (no confusing characters, readable over the phone). The guest presents it at the desk, shows the QR-coded confirmation slip, or just says it to the AI receptionist on WhatsApp, which looks up the live status instantly.
Should I switch from Cloudbeds, Mews or Little Hotelier?
If your guests live on WhatsApp, per-module pricing is eating your margin, or you want Wi-Fi, locks and IPTV inside the PMS — yes. StayChat includes the AI receptionist, POS-on-folio, double-entry accounting, guest currency geo-detection and all bookings on a real, instant booking page at one flat price. If you run a large group that mainly resells through OTAs and needs deep channel management across dozens of properties, a channel-heavy vendor may still add value on top — StayChat's direct engine is built to reduce that dependence, not replicate it.
How do I connect WhatsApp (Meta Developer Console)?
Create a Business-type app at developers.facebook.com and add the WhatsApp product. Copy the Phone Number ID and a permanent System User access token, pick any Webhook Verify Token string, and copy the App Secret — paste all four into Settings → WhatsApp, which also shows your exact webhook URL (/api/webhooks/whatsapp/<your-subdomain>). Then in Meta → WhatsApp → Configuration, set that callback URL + verify token and subscribe to the "messages" field. The full step-by-step guide is in the Help Center under "WhatsApp & the AI receptionist".
Can I sell event halls and pool time?
Yes. Add halls (day or hourly rate) and pools (per person per hour) and a Facilities tab appears on your public booking page automatically. Time-window conflicts are rejected live, and confirmations work exactly like room bookings.
Can two hotels share one installation?
Yes — StayChat OS is multi-tenant by design. Tenant isolation is enforced in a single middleware chokepoint, so hotels can never read each other's bookings, guests or credentials. A platform operator (superadmin) manages the portfolio.
